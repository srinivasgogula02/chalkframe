Marketing Site Design Brief: plusMinus
Positioning
Tagline: The Operating System for Your Identity. Core Value Prop: Stop guessing. Start tracking. Quantify your evolution with data-driven goals and daily focus. Target Audience: High-performers, disciplined individuals, and those seeking to rigorously improve their life through measurement.

Consumer Psychology & Strategy
Scarcity/Exclusivity: "Not for everyone. Only for those serious about growth."
Clarity & Control: Focus on the clean, distraction-free UI. The "plusMinus" concept implies binary simplicity—you either did it or you didn't.
Social Proof: Show a "Public Profile" example. "Prove your work."
Visual Language
Theme: "plusMinus" (Monochrome, High Contrast).
Colors: Deep Black (#000000), Pure White (#FFFFFF), Subtle Grays (#F5F5F5).
Typography:
Headings: Inter or Geist Sans (Bold, Tight Tracking) - "Premium & Modern".
Body: Inter (Clean, Readable).
Imagery:
High-fidelity screenshots of the App (Dashboard, Heatmap, Dark Mode).
Floating UI interface mockups (Glassmorphism or clean borders).
No stock people photos. Focus on the Interface as the hero.
Site Structure (Single Page Landing)
1. Hero Section
Headline: "Quantify Your Evolution."
Subheadline: "The ultimate dashboard to track your daily wins, long-term goals, and personal manifesto. Your life, measured."
CTA: [ Launch App app.plusminus.com ] (Primary) | [ View Demo ] (Secondary)
Visual: A tilted, 3D-perspective screenshot of the Dashboard showing a "Perfect Week" heatmap.
2. The "Problem" (Chaos vs Order)
Copy: "Most goals are vague wishes. plusMinus turns them into data."
Visual: Comparison of "Notes app list" (Messy) vs "plusMinus Dashboard" (Structured).
3. Feature Breakdown
Daily Focus: "Win the day. One simplified list."
The Manifesto: "Codify your principles. Track your adherence."
Wins vs Ls: "Honest tracking. Celebrate wins, own your Ls."
Public Profile: "Silence the impostor syndrome. Show your progress to the world."
4. Technical / "OS" Angle
Copy: "Built for speed. Local-first feel. Keyboard centric."
Visual: Terminal-like speed indicators or "Command Palette" visuals.
5. Footer
Simple links: Login, Pricing (if any), Twitter.
"Designed for 2026."
